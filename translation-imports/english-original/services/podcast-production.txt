Global Untold Story develops and produces audio and video podcasts for brands, companies, institutions, executives and creators.

BUILD A SHOW, NOT JUST AN EPISODE
Many podcasts begin with enthusiasm and stop after a few recordings.
The problem is rarely the equipment.
It is usually the absence of a clear format, realistic production workflow and long term content plan.
Before recording begins, we define:
Who the show is for
Why the audience should care
What makes the conversation distinctive
How each episode should be structured
How often the show can realistically be produced
What content should be created from every recording
How the show should develop over time
The result is a format that can remain consistent without making every episode feel the same.
